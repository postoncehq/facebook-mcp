# Facebook Page description

Write the copy that sits on a Facebook Page: the short intro (bio) and the longer About details. This skill writes the text; the user pastes it into their Page settings themselves. The PostOnce MCP publishes posts and can't edit Page profiles.

## Before writing

Get or infer: the business or creator name, what they offer, who it's for, where they are (for local businesses), what makes them different in concrete terms, contact details and the main link, and the action they want visitors to take (call, book, message, shop, follow). Never invent awards, years in business, ratings or client names.

## The short intro

This is the text that shows near the top of the Page. Facebook limits it to 101 characters, so check the count.

- Say what you do and for whom in plain words: "Sourdough bakery and baking classes in Portland."
- Add one concrete differentiator or proof if there's room: "Open 7am daily", "Family-run since 2012" (only if true).
- Use the words customers search: the service, the category, the town.
- No slogans that could fit any business.

## The longer About copy

Use this where the Page has space for more detail (description, services, story):

1. What you offer, in one or two sentences.
2. Who it's for and what they get.
3. Proof: specifics the user provides.
4. How to act: book, call, message, visit, with the link or hours.

Keep paragraphs short. Include the category and location keywords once or twice, naturally. No keyword lists.

## Also suggest

- The Page category that fits best (the user picks it in settings).
- The call-to-action button that matches the goal (Book now, Send message, Shop now, Call now).

## Output format

Give 3 short-intro options with character counts, then one longer About draft. Recommend an intro and say why in one line.

## Output

Tell the user to paste the copy in their Page's edit or settings screen. Offer next steps the MCP can do: plan Page posts with `facebook-content-calendar`, or publish a post now with the `postonce` skill.
